# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-5621 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Catering order/event record logging, event scheduling, supply-order
coordination, food-safety-concern escalation.
Out-of-scope: Directly finalizing food-safety-authority decisions
(allergen-exclusion overrides, kitchen safety certification), health-department/
regulatory clearances, product-recall decisions, food-service license
suspension/revocation.
